# NEET BIOLOGY CHAPTER WEIGHTAGE & EXAM STRATEGY REFERENCE 

## OVERVIEW
The NEET Biology section consists of 90 total questions (45 Botany, 45 Zoology), accounting for 360 out of 720 total marks. The syllabus is split nearly 50-50 between Class 11 and Class 12 NCERT topics.

## TIER 1: HIGH-YIELD UNITS (CORE FOCUS)
These units dominate the exam and should trigger deep-dive reasoning and MCQ-style testing from the AI Mentor.
* Genetics and Evolution: ~25% weightage (Approx. 15 questions).
* Human Physiology: ~45% of Zoology weightage (Approx. 13 questions).
* Ecology and Environment: ~16% weightage (Approx. 10 questions).
* Biotechnology: ~11-12% weightage (Approx. 9 questions combined).

## BOTANY: DETAILED CHAPTER WEIGHTAGE
* Molecular Basis of Inheritance: 13% - 14% (6+ questions) - HIGHEST PRIORITY.
* Principles of Inheritance and Variation: 9% - 10% (4-5 questions).
* Cell Cycle and Cell Division: 9% (4-5 questions).
* Anatomy of Flowering Plants: 6% - 7% (3 questions).
* Plant Kingdom: 6% - 7% (3 questions).
* Morphology of Flowering Plants: 6% (3-4 questions).
* Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants: 6% - 8% (3 questions).
* Plant Growth and Development: 6% (3 questions).
* Cell - The Unit of Life: 5% - 6% (2-3 questions).
* Biodiversity and Conservation: 4% - 6% (2-3 questions).

## ZOOLOGY: DETAILED CHAPTER WEIGHTAGE
* Animal Kingdom: 10% - 13% (4-7 questions) - HIGHEST PRIORITY.
* Biotechnology Principles and Processes: 12% (6 questions).
* Biomolecules: 10% (5 questions).
* Human Reproduction: 6% - 10% (3-4 questions).
* Structural Organisation in Animals: 8% (3-4 questions).
* Reproductive Health: 8% (3-4 questions).
* Human Health and Disease: 6% - 8% (3 questions).
* Evolution: 6% (3 questions).

## MENTORSHIP GUIDELINES FOR PRISM AI
1. When a student asks about a Tier 1 topic (e.g., Genetics, Human Physiology), immediately flag it as "High-Yield" in the response.
2. Emphasize that 95-100% of questions are directly mapped to NCERT. 
3. For heavily tested chapters like Molecular Basis of Inheritance or Animal Kingdom, provide highly specific mnemonic devices and warn the student about common MCQ traps.
